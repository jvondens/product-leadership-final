# Product Strategy & OKRs: Meridian Foundations

> Module 1 · Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test

## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration** | Meridian is the construction project-management platform of choice that enables complex, multi-site project tracking and reporting in a way that is not intrusive or obstructive to the jobs that need to get done by the teams on the ground. It meets the user where they are, whether that's the project managers and finance teams who need it for reporting, or the superintendents and foremen closest to the work, because of the way it seamless integrates into the flow of work. |
| **Where to play** | Primary focus will be winning the field, beginning with 3 primary workflows: Daily reports, safety observations and issue escalation. This creates value for the amins while also giving construction teams a direct line of communication up on important topics like safety and issues. |
| **How to win** | Meridian will win the field by becoming the fastest way for superintendents and foremen to capture, escalate, and resolve job-site issues from mobile, starting with issue escalation and extending into daily reports and safety observations only where those workflows reduce field burden and improve downstream enterprise reporting without duplicate entry. |
| **Capabilities required** | Deep understanding of workflows and technology on the ground at construction sites |
| **Management systems** | Adoption rates, weekly usage statistics, and NPS |

## 2. Your one hard no

> **Hard no:** We will not build net-new administrative complexity unless it directly increases field adoption, reduces duplicate entry, or improves the value of field-generated data.

## 3. OKR cascade

> **Objective:** Become the construction project-management platform of choice for the superintendents and foremen closest to the work.
>
> - **KR1:** 80% of active projects have weekly field updates submitted by superintendents or foremen
> - **KR2:** 85% of job-site photos/issues are captured in Meridian instead of external channels
> - **KR3:** 65% of field users repeat the core workflow weekly for 12 weeks

## 4. AI pressure-test

**Most valid challenge:** I appreciate that the AI challenged the specificity of what I'm going after. It challenged me to narrow in from "winning the field" to specific workflows such as daily reports, safety observations, issue escalation, etc. It also challenged my "why" behind adoption lacking. Whereas I assumed it was a UX issue, the AI suggested it could be a trust, incentive, or perceived surveillance challenge.

**What I will change and what I will defend:** I maintain that we should be developing something that integrates into the existing flow of work without creating administrative burden or overhead. I will go back however and make edits to narrow the initial focus to 2-3 workflows. I will also make what we will NOT be focused on more specific.
